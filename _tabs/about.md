I'm a security engineer who works on penetration testing and architecture reviews, and spends a lot of spare time breaking AI systems.

This blog is where I write up what I learn: AI red teaming, prompt injection and agent security, and the web application bugs that AI agents can be talked into reaching. Most posts walk through a lab or a challenge step by step, including the dead ends, and end with what defenders can do about it.

**What you'll find here**

- Write-ups of AI and LLM security labs and competitions
- Notes on testing AI agents, scanners and tool-using models
- Web application pentest techniques, especially where they meet AI

**Certifications:** GPEN, GCIH, GSEC

All testing described here was done in labs, competitions or environments I was authorised to test. Everything on this site is my own and doesn't represent any employer or client.
